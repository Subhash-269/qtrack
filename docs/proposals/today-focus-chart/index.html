Month = this calendar month so far. Custom opens two date fields. Days with no focus stay as empty slots so gaps are visible.

What counts

  • Focus sessions only: timer pomodoros and ones logged by an assistant.
  • Breaks, meetings and waiting / interrupted pauses are left out, as in Focus stats.
  • A session counts in the hour (or day) it ended.

Controls

  • Range: Today (default) · Week · Month · Custom.
  • Projects: All, or tick a few. "All" clears the ticks.
  • Both are remembered on this device.

Kept minimal

  • No axes beyond hour/day labels and two faint guide lines.
  • Totals live in the header and the legend; details on hover.
